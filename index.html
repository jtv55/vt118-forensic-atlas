The Speeding Silver Civic (23:33 PM), James Robitaille's Overnight Accident Sighting, & Our 2004 Forum Candidates

Everything on VT-118 that night is bracketed by two eyewitness accounts of Brianna's 1985 Oldsmobile 88 backed into the abandoned Dutchburn farmhouse: first, the **2025 witness couple** who passed four times between 23:00 and 23:35 and chased a **silver/grey Civic-like compact** that braked hard at the house and fled west at **65+ mph**; and second, ex-boyfriend **James Robitaille** (since deceased), who drove past later that night, saw what looked like an **accident** with the headlights burning, a directional blinking, and **both front doors open**, and shut the doors and turned off the lights without calling police.

Witness Anchor 1 · 23:00–23:35 EST (Friday Night) Grade B · Missing Ep. 26 (2025)

The 4 Passes, the Man in the Headlights, & the 65+ mph Silver Civic Flight

  • • Pass 1 (~23:00 E) & Pass 2 (~23:30 W): At 23:00 the house is empty. At ~23:30 Brianna's Oldsmobile is backed into the west gable, headlights on, both doors closed, nobody visible.
  • • Pass 3 (~23:32 E): After turning around at a covered bridge, they see one car door open and a tall, stocky man in a black hoodie standing in her headlights, hiding his face.
