Why the Acura 1.6 EL / 1.7 EL matters

The Canadian badge-engineered Honda Civic sedan

From 1997 to 2005, Honda Canada sold a badge-engineered Honda Civic sedan exclusively in Canada under the name Acura 1.6 EL (6th-gen, 1997–2000) and Acura 1.7 EL (7th-gen, 2001–2005). To any American witness on Route 118 at night, a silver or titanium-grey Acura EL is indistinguishable from a Honda Civic sedan.

28 February 2003 · Lowell, Vermont (Across Hazen's Notch)

The 250-lb "Quebec Gold" helicopter drop onto a snowmobile trail

Just 24 km southeast of Montgomery Center across Hazen's Notch (VT-58) in Lowell, Vermont, US Border Patrol and DEA agents intercepted a brazen winter air-to-snowmobile smuggling drop on February 28, 2003 (`lowell-helicopter`):
